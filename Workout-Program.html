Training Program

Cut & sculpt · delts · traps · arms — 3-day (45–54, back & knee safe)

Read this once — it's the whole strategy

Definition comes from the kitchen, not the gym. Your meal plan + the calorie deficit (and Reta) strip the fat. This program's job is to build and defend the muscles that make you look shredded — big 3D delts, thick traps, full arms — so as the fat drops, those pop. Don't expect the weights to "tone"; expect them to hold and grow the showpieces while you diet.

The split (3 days/week): Day 1 Shoulders/Traps/Triceps · Day 2 Back/Rear-delts/Biceps · Day 3 Chest/Legs/Core. Your priority muscles (delts, traps, biceps, triceps) get hit hard and often; legs are trained once to maintain — that's the correct trade-off for your goal and your knees/back.

How hard: take every working set to 1–2 reps in reserve (RIR) — you could just about grind 1–2 more. Only the very last set of an isolation move goes to true failure. Control the lowering (2–3 sec down) on everything — that's where growth and joint safety both live.

Rest: ~2 min on presses/rows, 60–90 sec on isolation. Progress: when you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add a little weight next time (double progression). Deload every 6th week: same exercises, half the sets, ~2 RIR — your joints at 45–54 need it.

Cardio for the shred (not optional): on 2–3 non-lifting days do 30–45 min incline-treadmill walking or bike (zone 2, easy on knees & back) + aim ~8–10k steps daily. That, plus the diet, is what reveals the muscle.

Warm up every joint before the first heavy set — at our age that's the difference between training for years and being sidelined. Each day has a warm-up drop-down. Not medical advice; clear the load with your practitioner given the L5/S1 and eGFR notes.
